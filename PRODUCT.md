# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The campaign team and candidate behind a Maipú 2025 electoral run. Strategists, a data lead, and the candidate open this to read electoral results, padrón (voter roll) breakdowns, and field-survey ("relevamiento") findings, sliced by barrio. They are usually time-pressed and goal-driven: comparing neighborhoods, spotting where to invest effort, and forming a decision before the next meeting. Not a public transparency portal, not a generic municipal tool, an internal decision instrument.

## Product Purpose

Turn raw Google Sheets data (electoral, census/indicators, voter roll, field surveys) into fast, trustworthy reads that drive campaign decisions. Success is a strategist reaching a defensible conclusion in seconds: which barrios are winnable, where turnout or sentiment is soft, which problems residents raise most. The dashboard exists to compress analysis time and surface signal, not to archive data.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, precise, confident. A private analyst instrument, not a civic brochure. Three words: incisive, composed, data-forward. The reference lane is Linear / Stripe / Datadog: the numbers are the protagonist, the chrome recedes, and confidence comes from typographic discipline and restraint rather than decoration. It should feel like a tool built by people who respect the reader's intelligence and time.

## Anti-references

- **The generic navy-blue government dashboard** (its current state): navy sidebar + sky-blue accent + white rounded cards. This is the civic-data training-data reflex and the primary thing to move away from.
- Cluttered enterprise admin templates (AdminLTE / Bootstrap), everything boxed in identical cards.
- Playful, bubbly consumer aesthetics; emoji-driven UI.
- Anything that reads as "official municipal portal."

## Design Principles

- **The data is the protagonist.** Every pixel earns its place by improving comprehension of the numbers. Chrome, containers, and ornament recede.
- **Decision speed over decoration.** Surfaces are built so a strategist reaches a conclusion fast: comparison, ranking, and meaningful alerting beat pretty arrangements.
- **Earned confidence, not civic cliché.** Looks like a sharp private analyst tool. Actively reject the navy-blue gov reflex and the "official portal" register.
- **Honest signal.** Alerts, trends, and emphasis are proportionate and meaningful, never decorative. No fake hero metrics, no alarm styling on non-alarms.
- **Density without clutter.** High information density is handled through rhythm, alignment, and hierarchy, not boxes nested in boxes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Spanish-language (es-AR) throughout: number, date, and percentage formatting follow es-AR. Data visualization must be colorblind-safe (do not encode meaning by hue alone; pair color with label, shape, or position), since charts carry the core message. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Readable on a laptop in a campaign office and on a phone in the field.
